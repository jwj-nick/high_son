## 핵심 학습 영역

### 1. Essay Structure (구조)
- **Intro**: Background info + a good hook (질문/통계/명언) + Your opinion (= 주장)
- **Body**: Reason #1 + Supporting details / Reason #2 + Supporting details
- **Conclusion**: Summary of main points + Powerful ending (인사이트·시사점)

### 2. 필수 문법 3종 ⭐
| # | 구조 | 패턴 | 예문 (AI 맥락) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **관계대명사 (Relative Clause)** | who / which / that | Students **who** use AI tools should ... |
| 2 | **가주어-진주어 (It ~ to/that ~)** | It is + 형용사 + to V / that S+V | **It is** necessary **to** prepare for future changes. / **It is** clear **that** AI will affect education. |
| 3 | **분사 (Participle)** | Ving (현재분사) / Past Participle (과거분사) | Students **using** AI tools should be careful. / Data **collected** by companies can be dangerous. |

→ 모든 모범 essay에 3종 모두 포함되어야 함.
