# E1 모범 Essay — Human Abilities AI Cannot Replace

> **Topic**: Since AI can provide information easily, what abilities should humans develop that AI cannot replace?

---

## 모범답안 (≈ 215 words)

**In my opinion**, in the age of AI, humans should focus on three abilities that machines cannot replicate: **critical thinking, creativity, and compassion**. **❶ It is true that** AI can recall facts more quickly than any human, but knowing is no longer the same as understanding.

**First**, critical thinking allows us to question AI itself. Students **❷ who** simply trust AI-generated answers risk repeating errors and biases. **For example**, large language models often invent fake sources, which is called "hallucination." **Therefore**, learning how to verify, compare, and evaluate information becomes more important than memorizing it.

**Furthermore**, creativity and compassion — qualities **❸ rooted** in human experience — give meaning to knowledge. An artist's painting, a teacher's encouragement, or a doctor's empathy cannot be produced by code. **According to** the World Economic Forum's 2023 report, "analytical thinking, creative thinking, and AI literacy" are the most valuable skills for 2027 — all of which require uniquely human judgment.

**On the other hand**, basic literacy and numeracy still matter, because they form the foundation for thinking critically about AI's output.

**In conclusion**, knowledge is now a commodity that anyone can access; judgment, imagination, and humanity are the new luxuries. **In short**, AI tells us *what is*, but only humans can decide *what should be*.
