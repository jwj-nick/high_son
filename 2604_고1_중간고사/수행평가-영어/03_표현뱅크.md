# 03 표현 뱅크 — STEP2 표현 50+ 카테고리별 + 예문

> 시험장에서 변형해 쓸 수 있도록, 각 표현마다 AI 주제 예문 1개씩.
> 모두 100% 외우려 하지 말고, **카테고리당 2-3개 핵심만** 골라서 자유자재로.

---

## 1. 자기 주장 (Introduction) — 7종

### 긍정 (5종)
| # | 표현 | 한글 뉘앙스 | AI 주제 예문 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I agree that ~ | (가장 무난) ~에 동의한다 | I agree that AI will reshape the future of work. |
| 2 | I firmly believe that ~ | 강하게 믿는다 | I firmly believe that schools must adapt to AI. |
| 3 | I strongly support the idea that ~ | ~라는 생각을 강력 지지 | I strongly support the idea that governments should regulate AI. |
| 4 | In my opinion, ~ | (가장 흔한 시작) 내 생각엔 | In my opinion, creativity is what humans must preserve. |
| 5 | I maintain that ~ | (다소 formal) ~라고 주장 | I maintain that personal data must be protected. |

### 부정 (2종)
| # | 표현 | AI 주제 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | I disagree that ~ | I disagree that AI will replace all human jobs. |
| 7 | I question whether ~ | I question whether AI development should remain unrestricted. |

> 💡 **추천**: "In my opinion, ~" / "I firmly believe that ~" — 두 개만 입에 붙이면 충분.

## 2. 이유 나열 (Body) — 10종

| # | 표현 | 위치 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **First, ~** | 첫 번째 이유 |
| 2 | Second, ~ | 두 번째 |
| 3 | Third, ~ / Finally, ~ | 세/마지막 |
| 4 | **First of all, ~** | 시작 강조 |
| 5 | **To begin with, ~** | 시작 |
| 6 | To start with, ~ | 시작 |
| 7 | **Furthermore, ~** | 추가 |
| 8 | **Moreover, ~** | 게다가 |
| 9 | In addition, ~ | 게다가 |
| 10 | Further, ~ | 또한 |

**예문 세트 (W1 AI & Jobs 가정)**:
- **First**, AI will eliminate many routine jobs such as data entry and basic translation.
- **Moreover**, new types of work — AI ethicists, prompt engineers — will emerge.

> 💡 **추천**: 한 글에 "First ~ / Second ~" 또는 "First ~ / Furthermore ~" 조합. 다 쓰지 말 것.

## 3. 예시 (Body) — 4종

| # | 표현 | AI 주제 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **For example, ~** | For example, ChatGPT can write essays in seconds. |
| 2 | For instance, ~ | For instance, self-driving cars rely on machine learning. |
| 3 | As evidence, ~ | As evidence, a 2023 OECD report showed ... |
| 4 | In support of this, ~ | In support of this, recent studies indicate ... |

> 💡 **추천**: "For example, ~" 가 가장 자연스러움. **출처 문장은 "According to ~ ..." 형식** 추가 (아래 별도 §5 참조).

---

## 4. 인과 (Cause / Effect) — 9종

### 원인 표시 (앞에 옴)
| # | 표현 | 용법 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Since** S+V, ~ | (이유) Since AI handles data quickly, ... |
| 2 | **Because** S+V / Because of N | Because AI tools are widely available, ... |
| 3 | Due to N | Due to rapid AI growth, ... |
| 4 | For this reason, ~ | (앞 문장 받아서) For this reason, schools must change. |

### 결과 표시 (앞 문장 받아서)
| # | 표현 | 예문 |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Consequently, ~** | Consequently, traditional jobs are disappearing. |
| 6 | Accordingly, ~ | Accordingly, students should learn new skills. |
| 7 | **Therefore, ~** | Therefore, regulation is necessary. |
| 8 | This results in ~ | This results in major changes in education. |
| 9 | **As a result, ~** | As a result, demand for creative thinkers grows. |

> 💡 **추천**: 원인 = "Since ~,"  / 결과 = "Therefore, ~" or "As a result, ~"

## 5. 비교·대조·양보 (Compare / Contrast) — 13종

### 비교 (Similarly) — 4종
- **Similarly, ~**
- In like manner, ~
- In the same way, ~
- Likewise, ~

### 대조 (Contrast) — 6종
| # | 표현 | 사용처 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **On the other hand, ~** | 반대 측면 |
| 2 | **On the contrary, ~** | 정반대 |
| 3 | As opposed to N | (전치사구) |
| 4 | **Nevertheless, ~** | 그럼에도 |
| 5 | Conversely, ~ | 반대로 |
| 6 | Yet, ~ | (짧고 강함) |

### 양보 (Although) — 3종
- **Even though** S+V, ~ — Even though AI is useful, it cannot replace empathy.
- **Rather than** N/Ving, ~ — Rather than fearing AI, we should learn to use it.
- **In spite of** N, ~ — In spite of its risks, AI offers great benefits.

> 💡 **추천**: "On the other hand, ~" + "Even though ~" 조합 1쌍이면 충분.

## 6. 강조 — 3종

- **Significantly, ~** (눈에 띄게)
- **Notably, ~** (특히)
- **Importantly, ~** (중요한 것은)

> 💡 한 글에 1번만. 너무 자주 쓰면 어색.

## 8. 출처 인용 (자료 사용) — 5종 [추가]

> STEP2 표에는 없지만 페이지 3에 "SOURCE 출처" 칸이 있어 실전에서 필수.

| # | 표현 | 예시 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **According to N, ~** | According to a 2023 OECD report, ... |
| 2 | A recent study by N shows that ~ | A recent study by McKinsey shows that 30% of jobs will change by 2030. |
| 3 | Research indicates that ~ | Research indicates that AI literacy improves academic performance. |
| 4 | Data from N reveal(s) that ~ | Data from the World Economic Forum reveal that ... |
| 5 | As reported by N, ~ | As reported by The Guardian, ... |

> 💡 **추천**: "According to ~, ~" 한 개만 외워두기. 본문에 1회 박으면 점수 안정.
