# W2 모범 Essay — Jobs That Still Need Humans

> **Topic**: What kinds of jobs do you think will still need humans in the future? Why?

---

## 모범답안 (≈ 210 words)

**In my opinion**, three types of jobs will always need humans: caregiving roles, creative professions, and ethical decision-makers. **❶ It is obvious that** machines can process information faster than people, but they cannot replicate the heart and judgment of a human being.

**First**, jobs **❸ involving** emotional connection — such as teachers, nurses, and counselors — will remain essential. A teacher does more than transfer knowledge; she notices when a student is sad or struggling. **For example**, the comfort a nurse gives a frightened patient cannot be programmed into an algorithm.

**Furthermore**, creative professions **❷ which** require original imagination will continue to belong to humans. Writers, designers, and chefs invent new ideas by drawing on personal experience and culture, something AI can only imitate.

**On the other hand**, jobs based on repetitive calculation may disappear. **Nevertheless**, the World Economic Forum's 2023 report states that "analytical thinking, creativity, and AI literacy" will be the most valuable skills by 2027 — all of which depend on human ability.

**In conclusion**, the jobs that will survive are not those that compete with AI, but those that complete what AI cannot. **In short**, what makes us irreplaceable is not what we know, but what we feel and create.
