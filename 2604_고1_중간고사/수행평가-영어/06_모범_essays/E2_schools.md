# E2 모범 Essay — Schools Should Change

> **Topic**: Do you think schools should change what they teach to prepare students for the future?  
> **Stance**: Yes, schools must significantly change.

---

## 모범답안 (≈ 220 words)

**I strongly support the idea that** schools should change what they teach. **❶ It is undeniable that** today's students will work in jobs that do not yet exist, using technologies that have not been invented. **Therefore**, education must evolve too.

**First**, students **❷ who** only memorize facts will struggle in a world where AI provides any answer instantly. Schools should focus instead on critical thinking, problem solving, and AI literacy — the ability to use, question, and create with AI responsibly. **For example**, South Korea will introduce AI-based digital textbooks for math, English, and information starting in 2025.

**Furthermore**, skills **❸ involving** collaboration, ethics, and creativity must be central. **According to** the World Economic Forum's 2023 report, "analytical thinking, creative thinking, and resilience" are the most important skills for the coming years. **As a result**, project-based and interdisciplinary learning will matter more than rigid subject lectures.

**On the other hand**, basic literacy, numeracy, and discipline remain essential. **Nevertheless**, the goal is not to discard the old curriculum but to update it for a new era.

**In conclusion**, schools cannot prepare students for the future by teaching only the past. **In brief**, education must change — or risk becoming irrelevant in the age of AI.
