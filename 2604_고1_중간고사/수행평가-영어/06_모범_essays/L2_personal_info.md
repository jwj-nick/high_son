# L2 모범 Essay — Personal Information Online

> **Topic**: How much personal information do you think people should share online?  
> **Stance**: People should share as little personal information online as possible.

---

## 모범답안 (≈ 215 words)

**In my opinion**, people should share as little personal information online as possible. **❶ It is dangerous to** treat the internet as a private diary, because every post can be stored, copied, and traced forever.

**First**, data **❸ collected** by companies is often used without our full understanding. Photos, searches, and even location histories are turned into profiles that advertisers and sometimes governments can access. **For example**, a single childhood post on social media can affect college admissions or job interviews years later.

**Furthermore**, oversharing increases the risk of cybercrime. **According to** the IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report 2023, the average data breach costs about $4.45 million, and Statista estimates the global cost of cybercrime at around $8 trillion in 2023. **Therefore**, even ordinary users can become targets of identity theft or scams.

**On the other hand**, some sharing is necessary for connection and self-expression. **Even though** social media offers real benefits, users **❷ who** post personal details without limits expose themselves to long-term harm.

**In conclusion**, the safest principle is "share less, protect more." **In short**, your data is your identity — once it is shared, it is no longer yours alone.
