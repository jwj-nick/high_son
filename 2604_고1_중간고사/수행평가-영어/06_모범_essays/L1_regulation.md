# L1 모범 Essay — Government Regulation of AI

> **Topic**: Do you think governments should regulate AI development? Why or why not?  
> **Stance**: Yes, governments should regulate AI — but in a balanced way.

---

## 모범답안 (≈ 220 words)

**I firmly believe that** governments should regulate AI development to protect public safety and human rights. **❶ It is essential that** society sets clear rules before, not after, harmful consequences appear.

**First**, AI systems **❷ which** make critical decisions — about hiring, loans, or even prison sentences — can carry hidden biases. Without regulation, citizens have no way to challenge unfair outcomes. **For example**, several studies have shown that some facial recognition algorithms misidentify people of color far more often than others.

**Moreover**, personal data **❸ collected** by AI companies can be misused on a massive scale. **According to** the European Union's AI Act, which took effect in 2024, AI systems are now classified by risk level, and the most dangerous uses, such as social scoring, are completely banned. **As a result**, the EU has become the world's first jurisdiction with comprehensive AI rules.

**On the other hand**, some argue that strict regulation slows down innovation. **Nevertheless**, history proves that responsible technology — from airplanes to medicines — benefits from safety standards.

**In conclusion**, AI is too powerful to remain unregulated. Innovation without ethics is dangerous, but regulation without innovation is stagnant. **In brief**, the goal of regulation is not to stop AI but to make sure it serves humanity.
