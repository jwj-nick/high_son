### Q8. [3.9점]

Now, let me share the experiences of some people I interviewed. David, a chef and kitchen manager, who has been writing morning pages for two years, says:

> *My job tends to be very stressful. When I started writing morning pages, I just wrote a lot of complaints about my hard life. Later, I began to ask questions, like "What would make my life easier today?" "How can I improve my recipes?" Sometimes, I keep asking questions until I reach a sort of "click moment." It feels like a door opening to great ideas. Nowadays, I feel less stressed, enjoy my job more, and am more focused on what I truly want!*

I could totally understand David because I had gone through a similar experience. Like David, I asked many questions, for instance, "Why was I so angry yesterday?" "Why are these days less fun these days?" Then, I started raising more challenging ones: "What could make me happier right now?" "What is my ideal future? Can I reach it? How?"

Answering these questions showed me what kind of woman I was and what my needs, values, passions, and talents were. More importantly, I came to know what I truly wanted! I remembered my childhood dreams of being a writer, songwriter, and radio host. Gradually, vague ideas became clear plans. Years later, here I am, a successful podcaster! I've also published a book and a music album. My morning pages, which I still write every day, have given me the drive and ideas to achieve these aims.

① It has been two years since David started writing morning pages.
② From time to time, David is able to reach a click moment by continuing to ask himself questions.
③ The interviewer found it hard to relate to David, as her experience was quite different from his.
④ The interviewer gradually began to ask herself more challenging questions.
⑤ Even after becoming a successful podcaster, the interviewer still writes morning pages every day.

### Q9. [3.8점]

By now you've no doubt heard about Chandra's surprise party and are probably wondering why you weren't invited. I completely understand if you felt hurt or left out when you heard the news. Chandra certainly was: as soon as she arrived, she asked me, "Where's Christy?" The look on her face made it clear just how much she had been expecting to see you there. Your absence from the guest list is entirely my fault, and I'm truly sorry for my thoughtlessness. I reviewed the guest list several times, and I still cannot explain how your name simply was forgotten. I know how much Chandra values your friendship, and I certainly meant no offense. You have always been such an important part of her life, which makes my mistake all the more unforgivable. Even now, I still don't understand how I could have overlooked your name. It's a case, I suppose, of missing the obvious, of not seeing what's right before your eyes. I only hope that this unfortunate oversight does not damage the warm relationship you and Chandra have all shared for so long. Can you ever forgive me?

① Chandra asked about Christy's absence as soon as she arrived.
② Christy was not invited to Chandra's party despite being her close friend.
③ The writer left Christy's name off the guest list on purpose from the start.
④ The writer went over the guest list many times but still failed to include Christy's name.
⑤ The writer hopes that this careless mistake will not harm the long friendship Christy and Chandra have shared.
