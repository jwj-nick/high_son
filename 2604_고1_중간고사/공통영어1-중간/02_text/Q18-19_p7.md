### Q18. 주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것은? [4.1점]

> Harvard business professor Alison Wood Brooks has done studies that show how stress and anxiety change based on what we tell ourselves. Brooks's experiment sounds like a blast or a nightmare, depending on your perspective.

(A) The decision to tell themselves that the feeling they were experiencing was excitement helped them convert the energy into something positive, even useful.

(B) She took a group of subjects with stage fright to a crowded bar with a stage and had them get up there and sing. All the subjects arrived nervous, as expected.

(C) She had one nervous group tell themselves over and over, "I am excited," and tested them against a control group who just sat with their nerves and their regular self-talk. The results were eye-opening.

(D) But Brooks measured whether or not having them change their language — calling their nerves "excitement" — made any difference.

① (A) – (C) – (B) – (D)
② (B) – (A) – (D) – (C)
③ (B) – (D) – (C) – (A)
④ (C) – (B) – (D) – (A)
⑤ (C) – (D) – (A) – (B)
