### [Q2~Q3] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.

Nothing about an organization's strategy or business model is absolute. But there must be a cutting point between principles and values and everything else. They represent precious, freestanding assets that must be **(A)** [free / full] of strategy. They provide continuity and identity when everything else is **(B)** [essential / disposable]. They represent the core element of the culture and the unchanging soul of the organization. Cases in which leaders have successfully remodeled an entire organization represent organizational change in its comprehensive and supreme category. We learn from these cases that retaining principles and values during the process of change is not only possible but essential to provide an **(C)** [anchor / obstacle]. Ironically, perhaps, organizations with the strongest principles and values often have the highest adaptive capacity because people attach themselves to them and understand that everything else is negotiable. If you want to keep your promises, burn the house when it's time to reinvent the company. But save the nails.
