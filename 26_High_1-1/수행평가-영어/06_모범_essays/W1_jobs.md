# W1 모범 Essay — AI & Jobs

> **Topic**: Do you think AI will take away many more jobs or create new opportunities for people?  
> **Stance**: AI creates more opportunities than it takes away.

---

## 모범답안 (≈ 215 words)

**In my opinion**, AI will create more opportunities than it takes away. **❶ It is clear that** artificial intelligence is reshaping our economy at an unprecedented pace, but history shows that new technologies eventually generate more jobs than they destroy.

**First**, jobs **❸ requiring** creativity, empathy, and ethical judgment cannot be easily automated. Teachers, counselors, designers, and healthcare workers rely on uniquely human qualities that algorithms cannot replicate. **For example**, a nurse must comfort a frightened patient, and a teacher must understand the emotional state of each student.

**Furthermore**, AI itself is generating new types of work. **According to the World Economic Forum's Future of Jobs Report 2023**, approximately 69 million new roles will appear by 2027 — including AI specialists, data ethicists, and prompt engineers. **As a result**, demand for human-AI collaboration skills is growing rapidly.

**On the other hand**, workers **❷ who** only perform routine tasks may face displacement. **Therefore**, lifelong learning is essential for everyone, regardless of profession.

**In conclusion**, AI is not the end of work but its transformation. The future belongs to those who can do what AI cannot — imagine, empathize, and create.
